Healthcare Data Analyst

We are looking for a data analyst to join our clinical reporting team. You will turn hospital and patient data into reports and dashboards that help clinicians and managers make decisions.

Responsibilities: build and maintain reports in SQL and a BI tool, check data quality, work with clinical staff to define measures, and present findings to non-technical audiences.

Requirements: a degree in a quantitative or health-related field, three or more years of analytics experience, strong SQL, and an understanding of healthcare data standards.
